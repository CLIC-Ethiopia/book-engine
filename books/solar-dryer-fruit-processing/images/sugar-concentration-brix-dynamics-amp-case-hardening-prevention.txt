Vector cross-section comparison of two drying fruit slices under high temperature vs controlled temperature. Left image (Case Hardening): High initial heat (&gt;65°C) flashes surface moisture away, forming a rubbery, hardened outer sugar skin while the center remains wet and raw. Right image (Optimal Drying): Moderate initial heat (50-55°C) allows uniform moisture migration from center to outer skin, producing a soft, pliable, fully dry slice. Blueprint technical style.
