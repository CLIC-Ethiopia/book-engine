Engineering schematic of a Commercial Hybrid LPG (Propane) Auto-Booster System for a solar tunnel dryer. Labeled elements: Thermostatic Digital Controller -&gt; 12V Solenoid Gas Valve -&gt; Commercial LPG Burner Ring mounted inside intake duct.
