Comparative technical diagram showing solar fruit drying methods. Left panel: Direct Sun Exposure showing UV rays hitting sliced mangoes directly, causing pigment degradation (browning/bleaching) and Vitamin C destruction. Right panel: Indirect Solar Drying where a solar thermal collector heats air separately, sending only hot air into a shaded, UV-blocked drying cabinet, preserving bright orange fruit color and nutrient integrity.
